# Google OAuth verification video checklist

- Show AudienceOwn homepage and branding with `audienceown.com` visible in the browser.
- Open the public privacy, terms, and data-deletion pages and show the support contact.
- Sign in as the reviewer; explain that Google sign-in is separate from YouTube authorization.
- Select **Connect YouTube** and show the entire English consent screen.
- Zoom in on the exact `youtube.readonly` permission and explain why it is required.
- Show the successful return, selected channel identity, subscriber state, and synchronization status.
- Show public upload monitoring and verify there are no upload, edit, delete, comment, playlist, or channel-management actions.
- Open Connected Accounts, show the read-only badge and capabilities, then disconnect.
- Show the revocation result, stopped synchronization, stored-data deletion, `/data-deletion`, and the account-deletion route.

Suggested narration:

> AudienceOwn requests read-only YouTube access to identify the creator’s channel, synchronize public subscriber metrics, and monitor public uploads used in the creator’s audience-protection dashboard. AudienceOwn does not upload, edit, or delete YouTube content.

Do not expose credentials, tokens, developer tools containing sensitive values, or another creator’s data in the recording.
