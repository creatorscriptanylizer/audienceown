# Audience Protection Score

Formula version `audience-protection-v1` is deterministic and server-owned: 60 points from canonical recovery coverage, then 10 points each for a published recovery page, enabled Recovery Pass, verified official identity account, and verified backup identity account.

States are Strong protection (80–100), Good foundation (60–79), Needs improvement (30–59), and High exposure (0–29). Popularity, social engagement, platform audience counts, and the private identity trust score are excluded.
