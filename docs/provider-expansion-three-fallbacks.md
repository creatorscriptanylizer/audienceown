# Provider Expansion III fallbacks

Manual imports require exact canonical provider post URLs, a title, and creator-supplied or authoritative publication time. Imports remain unverified, approval-required, non-trust-bearing, and deduplicated by the existing detection pipeline.

Ownership fallback may use expiring hashed profile/post challenges, verified creator domains, signed manifests, or constrained operator review. URLs and display-name matches alone never verify ownership.
