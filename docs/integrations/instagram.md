# Instagram
Create a Meta app, configure `INSTAGRAM_*`, and register
`/api/integrations/instagram/callback`. Request `instagram_business_basic`.
Professional creator/business accounts and Meta review are required. Approved
deployments may poll or receive webhooks for images, video, Reels, and carousels.
Personal-account watching is unsupported. Reconnect after revoked grants.
Stage 8.3 automatic detection is restricted to explicitly selected Business or Creator professional accounts through an official login mode. Consumer accounts remain manual-only and unverified.
