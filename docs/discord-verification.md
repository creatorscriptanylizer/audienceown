# Discord verification

AudienceOwn verifies guild or announcement-channel authority through OAuth or a bot installation with owner, administrator, or manage-guild permission. Stable guild and channel IDs anchor identity. Guild management does not prove ownership of a person's Discord identity.

Only explicitly configured public invites may be shown. Member lists, private messages, history, and bot tokens are never ingested. Permission loss, removal, unavailable resources, invite changes, and renames are monitored; harmless renames preserve stable identity.
